THE PROBLEM

Ruangprint had built a solid reputation through repeat customers and word-of-mouth referrals. However, customer acquisition remained highly dependent on organic channels, making growth difficult to scale predictably. The business needed a system that could consistently generate qualified inquiries from business owners, SMEs, and local brands actively looking for printing services. Key challenges included: - Limited online visibility beyond existing customers. - No structured paid advertising strategy. - Creative assets focused on products rather than customer pain points. - Inconsistent lead flow from digital channels.

THE STRATEGY

To generate more qualified leads, I developed a targeted Meta Ads strategy focused on key customer segments such as SMEs, food & beverage businesses, fashion brands, and event organizers. Multiple creative concepts were tested, ranging from product showcases and promotional offers to problem-solution messaging, allowing campaigns to identify and scale the highest-performing ads. To maximize conversions, the customer journey was streamlined by directing prospects straight to WhatsApp, reducing friction and enabling faster communication with potential clients.

CREATIVE APPROACH

Rather than promoting printing products alone, the campaigns focused on business outcomes. The messaging emphasized how professional packaging, labels, and promotional materials could improve brand perception and customer trust. Visuals were designed to showcase real-world applications, helping potential customers immediately understand the value of the products for their own businesses.

THE RESULTS

Within the campaign period, the ads generated 1,812 clicks from 28,524 impressions while maintaining a strong 6.35% click-through rate (CTR). With a total advertising spend of approximately Rp1.25 million, the campaign successfully drove targeted traffic and increased awareness among potential customers. The results demonstrated the effectiveness of audience targeting and creative testing in attracting highly relevant prospects at an efficient cost. Within two months, Google Business performance grew by 92.8%, increasing total interactions from 14,699 in March to 28,335 in May across five branch locations.
