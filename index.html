The problem
Growth leaned on repeat customers and referrals. There was no structured paid strategy, the Google Business Profiles of every branch weren't optimised, creatives showed products instead of customer pain points, and leads from digital channels were inconsistent.
The strategy
I was brought in to grow the Google Business Profiles through Google Ads. I ran Performance Max and set it up end to end: headlines, promo offers, links, and the rest of the campaign settings. Alongside it, Meta Ads aimed at SMEs, F&B businesses, fashion brands, and event organizers tested product showcases, promo offers, and problem–solution messaging, scaled the winners, and sent prospects straight to WhatsApp.
Creative
Outcomes over services: what customers actually get out of it, shown in real-world use. For Performance Max, I wrote the headlines and promo assets myself.
The results
From January to October 2026, monthly Google Business Profile views across all branches grew 216%, about 3.2 times. Customer actions (calls, website clicks, and direction requests) grew 90%, almost double. On Meta Ads, the campaigns held a 6.35% CTR.
