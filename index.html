Featured Project

CASE
STUDY

Client
Vanta Beauty Co.
Year
2024
Scope
Branding + Meta Ads
Duration
3 Months

THE PROBLEM

Vanta Beauty had a quality product but a fragmented visual identity and no structured ad strategy. Conversion rates were below 1.2% and CPAs were unsustainably high at Rp 180,000 per customer on Meta Ads — making paid acquisition unprofitable at scale.

THE STRATEGY

We rebuilt the brand from the ground up — refining the visual identity, developing a cohesive content system, and restructuring their ad account with proper audience segmentation, funnel architecture, and creative testing frameworks.

CREATIVE APPROACH

Three-phase campaign: awareness using UGC-style video ads, consideration through editorial product photography, and conversion via direct-response carousels with strong offers. Each creative was A/B tested across four audience segments before full budget allocation.

THE RESULTS

Within 90 days, conversion rate climbed to 3.8%, CPA dropped 62%, and revenue from paid channels grew 247%. The brand also gained 28K new Instagram followers through organic content alignment — compounding the paid growth with an owned audience.
